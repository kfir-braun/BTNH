---
question: What's a sane way to store EV-tier power without a wall of batteries?
askedAt: '2026-08-15'
answeredAt: '2026-08-15'
---

Lapotronic Energy Orbs (or a bank of Lapotron Crystals in an ME system) get
you far more EU per block than stacking individual batteries in the world.
Route through them via a battery buffer multiblock so machines see stable
voltage even while the orb bank is charging/discharging.
